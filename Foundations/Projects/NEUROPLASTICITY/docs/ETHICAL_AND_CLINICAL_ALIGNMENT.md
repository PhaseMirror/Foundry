# Multiplicity Neuroplasticity: Ethical, Trauma-Aware, & Neurodiversity Alignment

This document outlines the clinical, ethical, and neurodiversity foundations of the Neuroplasticity model within Multiplicity Theory.

---

## 1. Neurodiversity-Centered Design (ASD & ADHD)

Traditional neuroplasticity paradigms often treat divergent cognitive processing as a deficit to be overwritten. Multiplicity Theory establishes a strengths-based, dignity-preserving framework:
- **EchoBraid Architecture:** Acknowledges that autistic and neurodivergent cognition often relies on deep phase-locked identity anchors (monotropic focus, sensory resonance). Rather than forcing uniform plasticity, EchoBraid decouples identity anchors ($p \in \{2, 3, 5\}$) from adaptive task channels ($p \in \{7, 11, 13, \dots\}$).
- **Sensory Rhythm Alignment:** Respects intrinsic oscillation frequencies, preventing overstimulation and sensory fatigue.

---

## 2. Trauma-Aware Homeostasis via CSL

Rapid cognitive change in the presence of unintegrated trauma or sensory overwhelm can trigger psychological fragmentation or panic.
- **The $\Delta S < \ln \varphi$ Guardrail:** Mathematically restricts maximum informational entropy dissipation in any single session.
- **Subjective Readiness Gating:** Uses EEG spectral power ratios (Alpha/Theta readiness vs Beta/Gamma stress) to scale learning velocity $\eta$. If the nervous system enters hyperarousal or distress, adaptation rate is automatically damped to prevent re-traumatization.

---

## 3. Non-Invasive Clinical Interfaces

The framework operates exclusively with non-invasive biofeedback and cognitive task interfaces:
- Real-time spectral EEG frequency monitoring.
- Closed-loop auditory/visual sensory pacing.
- Autonomic tone observation ensuring nervous system safety at every step of recursive learning.
