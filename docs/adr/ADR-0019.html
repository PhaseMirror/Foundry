ADR-0019: Technology Portfolio — Evidence, Risk, and Feasibility

Status: Accepted

Context

19 internal assets vs 15 public GitHub repositories. Gap between internal claims and externally verifiable evidence is standing risk.

Decision

Adopt evidence recut: Observe, Map, Interface method. Every asset classified Normative/Reference/Experimental/Historical. Evidence graded E0-E6 with dates, owners. 19-vs-15 deltas dispositioned with owners.

Consequences

  • Public claims downgraded to E0-E2 until independently evidenced
  • 19-vs-15 inventory deltas become action items with owners
  • UAC research claims excluded until partner queues exist
