ADR-0028: Target-First Observability Calculus — Admissible Ambiguity

Status: Accepted

Context

Design organized around available instrument rather than target claim is flawed. High-amplitude measurement may have zero target design gain; smaller measurement may close target.

Decision

Adopt target-first calculus: 13-step protocol. Residual ambiguity O_C(E) = C(ker A_E); design gain Δ_C(B|E) = d_C(E) − d_C(E∪B); practical margin μ_C(J_eff).

Consequences

  • Experiment selection by Δ_C > 0 or admissible-set contraction
  • Every claim carries declared target, state gate, forward map, nuisance, ambiguity, reversal, diagnostic, falsification
  • Three shortcuts forbidden for MnF₂ named explicitly
