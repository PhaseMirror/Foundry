ADR-006: MultiContract Atomic Contraction with PartialSum Tokens

Status: Accepted

Context

Contraction over sharded tensor dimensions produces partial sums requiring explicit collective reductions before downstream consumption.

Decision

Standardize opcode 0x05 (MultiContract) to atomically verify dual signatures and emit PartialSum(mesh_axis) tokens consumed strictly by subsequent Collective operations.

Consequences

  • Prevents unreduced partial sum escapes in SPMD graphs
  • Simultaneous atomic multi-axis tensor contractions in O(k) time
