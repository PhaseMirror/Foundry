# ADR-0015: Unified Civic Infrastructure Outline

**Status:** Accepted

## Context
Citizen Gardens needs a legal and operating scaffold that keeps the civic purpose distinct from the commercial operator while remaining lawful under Wyoming law. The governing instruments (Governing Principles v1.1) define purpose and caps; the civic layer must hold the literary/legal definition while the operator hosts the kernel and contracts with strangers. LawfulRecursionVersion 1.0 is the binding companion to Governing Principles v1.1.

## Decision
Adopt the Unified Civic Infrastructure Outline as the operating map for Citizen Gardens.

- **One sentence.** Citizen Gardens, a Wyoming unincorporated nonprofit association (DUNA-ready), grows governed operating capacity for the UOR civic layer under a two-legal-person wall.
- **The wall.** Two legal persons: (1) Citizen Gardens UNA — nonprofit, holds purpose, the literary definition of UCC, and remittance; (2) Phase Mirror operator LLC — hosts the kernel, keys, and contracts with strangers. WY UNA now (W.S. 17-22); DUNA at 100 members (W.S. 17-32).
- **Four infrastructures.** UNA/DUNA (legal persona); Buurtzorg topology (social operating structure); Bushidō (conduct code); Phase Mirror + Multiplicity Social Physics (machine-checked invariants: 2R+1 reciprocity, ELM credits, greenhouse living dividends, and no credit-bought votes).
- **Formation ladder.** EIN as an unincorporated association; Wyoming statement appointing agent; legal page live stating "Wyoming unincorporated nonprofit association. DUNA-ready." 501(c)(3) only when a grant or donor requires it, as a separate filing — not a costume change. Use "Citizen Gardens UNA"; do not yet use DUNA / DAO / DAO LLC labels.
- **Capital — four doors, no fifth.** Gift, sponsor, recoverable grant, and operator subscription. The greenhouse stake (living work accelerating to an LLC membership interest) is not a fifth door; it stays under UNA control, with Examiner veto and automatic PMCP.
- **Dual seat, practice and oracle.** The PMCP practitioner side is gated by the four gates (mastery interview, vertical playbook, compliance clearance, supervised engagement); the oracle owner side runs the Phase Mirror sentinel. PMCP failure does not unwind equity; revocation does not confiscate shares; using the mark after revocation is a separate breach. Yield lives only on the shop side — seats, packs, exam fees, and revenue share on delivered branded work. A certificate does not mint; a certificate attests.
- **Surplus pointed at purpose.** Excess is directed at the civic purpose per Governing Principles, never silently retained as operator yield.
- **Decision authority.** Roles and purchases are allocated by an explicit decision-authority table (who decides a role or a purchase), not by improvisation.
- **The playground.** Civic R&D runs its live Sovereignty Node on the UNA side; the engine is Phase Mirror + Multiplicity. L0 invariants are enforced machine-checked, not by human executive override.
- **Ninety-day sequence.** EIN, agent statement, and dual-control treasury first; then formation, capital doors, playground, and adoption in sequence, gated by the L0 invariants.
- **Named tensions, permanent.** UNA nonprofit vs SaaS revenue; 100-member floor vs early team. Resolution: UNA now; publish the count; do not claim DUNA.

## Consequences
* Legal identity is honest and forward-compatible: DUNA-ready without overclaiming.
* The wall keeps the kernel off the UNA balance sheet and the purpose off the operator's P&L.
* Credit is a recorded, machine-checked accounting unit (ELM credits), not a votable currency.
* Adoption is incremental and gated, so the civic layer never outruns its legal form.
