# ADR-0016: UOR Civic Infrastructure — Three Epochs

**Status:** Accepted

## Context
The Foundry's roadmap must be presentable as a physically and biologically constrained deployment rather than an abstract idea. The governing civic architecture (dense mathematics, PWEH integrity, triadic scaling) needs an epochal frame that a board can digest while preserving structural integrity: a formally verified trajectory of decreasing entropy that replaces traditional extractive growth.

## Decision
Adopt the Triadic Evolution model: the Foundry progresses through three strategic developmental epochs.

- **Epoch 1 — Foundation (Internal Spin-Up): Building the Core Engine.** Systemic state: mass density. The gateway gathers mass and establishes the Universal Atomic Calculator (UAC) and Phase Mirror core. Minting driver: staking, rewarding early contributors for the initial spin. Economic anchor: the Hundian Foundry State baseline of $1.00. Participants: Builders providing the core foundational mass.
- **Epoch 2 — Projection (Calibration): Aligning the External World.** Systemic state: thermalization — external reality aligned with internal logic. Spreads Citizen Gardens to build local resilience and projects Foundry spin outward. Minting driver: calibration; value is minted when a node successfully reduces regional dissonance, proving alignment with physics-grounded models. Economic anchor: stablecoin folds to $2.00 as multiplicity expands.
- **Epoch 3 — Maximum Multiplicity (Stewardship): The Self-Regulating Ecosystem.** Systemic state: dynamic equilibrium and self-regulating structural resonance. Triadic scaling 3 → 9 → 27 → 81 → 243 nodes. Minting driver: recursion and LifeBushido. Proof of practice: community maintenance is the computational proof that updates the network. Economic anchor: stablecoin reaches the Hundian limit of $3.00.
- **Epoch transitions are gated.** The jump from Epoch 1 to Epoch 2 is held to the strict 90-day Phase Mirror metric gates; entropy decreases only on verified evidence, not on schedule.

## Consequences
* Each epoch has exactly one systemic state, one minting driver, and one economic anchor — the model cannot be mutated mid-epoch without an explicit ADR.
* The $1.00 → $2.00 → $3.00 anchor progression is a policy constraint on the stablecoin layer, not a market forecast.
* Staking, calibration, and recursion+LifeBushido are sequential minting mechanisms; they do not run concurrently across epochs.
* Diffusion follows triadic powers of three; saturation at 243 bounds a single locus of counting.
