# ADR-0025: Static Multipolar Order and Dynamical Chiral Response — Probe–Tensor Correspondence in MnF₂

**Status:** Proposed

## Context
MnF₂ evidence spans elastic multipole reconstruction (polarized-neutron diffraction of anisotropic Mn magnetization and ligand spin polarization, a symmetry-allowed rank-5 magnetic multipole contribution) and inelastic magnon chirality (anti-symmetric dynamical correlation channel). Symmetry may relate these objects, but they are generated by different forward maps. The paper's central question is whether a static multipole functional can be inferred from a dynamical chiral measurement (or vice versa) purely from symmetry, and under what declared conditions rank identifiability holds at all.

## Decision
Adopt the probe–tensor correspondence as the discipline relating static multipolar and dynamical chiral claims in MnF₂.

- **Separated objects.** Hamiltonian coordinates, equilibrium one-point magnetization tensors, static response tensors, connected correlations, and the probe/instrument map are distinct mathematical objects and are never collapsed.
- **No automatic substitution.** A static multipole-rank functional f_K is identifiable from a dynamical chiral measurement M_χ only if f_K = f̄_K ∘ π_{M_χ}; symmetry compatibility alone does not establish this factorization. A polarized inelastic chiral signal does not by symmetry determine a static multipole rank, and a static multipolar reconstruction does not by itself prove the dynamical response of another probe.
- **Domain-ensemble caution.** An uncontrolled domain ensemble can produce a vanishing ensemble one-point moment coexisting with finite diffraction intensity; conversely opposite domains can cancel a sector in the measured average.
- **Conditional rank identifiability.** Identifiability of multipole rank is conditional on the declared basis, radial/form-factor model, origin convention, nuisance space, and domain state — none of which may be silently inherited.
- **No claim migration.** A seventh-neighbor exchange coefficient (S) is not a magnetic multipole (K), and a dynamical chiral correlator (χ) is not a direct static-rank measurement; each channel is closed or left open independently.

## Consequences
* Static-rank and dynamical-chirality results are kept as separate propositions, preventing cross-tensor claim migration (a prohibition formalized again in ADR-0027).
* Every multipole reconstruction states its conditioning assumptions or is not closed.
* Domain-cancellation and ensemble-averaging effects are handled explicitly rather than averaged silently.
* Establishes the K (static multipolar content) component used by the cross-material benchmark (ADR-0026).
