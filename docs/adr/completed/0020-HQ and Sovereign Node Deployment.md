# ADR-0020: HQ & Sovereign Node Deployment

**Status:** Completed

## Context
The UOR Foundation requires a physical ground state that operates as a LifeBushido Retreat, a working Sovereign Urban Gardens (SUG) node, and the site for the civic Sovereignty Node. The proposed location is 36.8 acres in Livermore, Larimer County, Colorado, under the Citizen Gardens UNA / Phase Mirror Protocol framework. Risk is to be aggressively mitigated by gating capital deployment into two operational phases. Social Physics is strict: M = 2R + 1 (reciprocity as sovereign infrastructure), triadic recursion 3 → 9 → 27, and Phase Mirror as a fail-closed sentinel enforcing L0 invariants without human executive override.

## Decision
Adopt the HQ & Sovereign Node executive proposal, conditioned on board authorization of the $750,000 capital and operational budget and on the two-phase gate structure.

- **Phase 1 — Ground-State Seeding (The Circle of 9).** Secure the 36.8-acre perimeter; deploy off-grid utilities (deep well, engineered septic, solar array); erect three 16-foot four-season yurts (one community amenity space, two bunkhouses). Launch a staggered 9-person Circle: three executives as meta-admins anchoring Triangles (Alex, CEO — Cybersecurity & L0 Invariants, Phase Mirror Sentinel, EchoMirror Academy; Ryan, COO — Ground-State Infrastructure & Build-out, Sovereign Urban Gardens, Defensive Publications; Adam, CFO — Energy Ledger & Commerce Firewall, Thermodynamic Optimization, Vitality & Embodied Health), each pulling in two paid interns ($15/hr + room and board) into Operator and Support roles. Triads: Admin (executive anchor), Operator (execution), Support (social soil).
- **Phase 2 — The UOR Foundry (The Family of 27).** Capital remains locked in the Foundation treasury until the Phase 1 team passes the Three-Way Test on a 90-day metric card: (1) Member Dignity — safe, regulated interpersonal dynamics and camera-free privacy zones; (2) Community Outcome — Phase 1 yurt infrastructure and off-grid utilities deployed; (3) Solvency — strict adherence to the 90-day treasury envelope without deficit. On passage, the remaining budget builds the 40x40 steel post-and-beam operational barn; the Circle scales by triadic recursion until the Hundian limit of 27 active operators, after which the Larimer County node is saturated and growth proceeds only by geographic split to seed a new node.
- **Governance and treasury safeguards.** Dual-control treasury — any disbursement over $2,500 requires dual-officer sign-off. Pauli exclusion — each functional role is capped at two occupants to prevent state crowding. Non-profit asset lock — land and physical structures vest permanently in the Foundation and cannot be extracted for private equity. Operational kill-switch — failing the 90-day metric card for two consecutive quarters mechanically halts local node spending.

## Consequences
* Capital is reversible only at preset gates; Phase 2 funds never leave the treasury on Phase 1 assumptions.
* The site cannot exceed 27 operators without seeding a new node — the node is bounded by design, not by negotiation.
* The three-way test is the only Phase 2 key; no executive override short of an ADR.
* Land and structures are locked to the Foundation; no private-equity route exists.
