# ADR-0018: Reinitialization — Executive Decision Brief

**Status:** Completed

## Context
UOR technology is ahead of UOR operating structure. The Foundation holds credible ontology, addressing, conformance, registry, application-model, and research assets; what is missing is one public source of truth, an approved authority chain, coordinated release ownership, independent implementation evidence, and attributable use outside Foundation-authored work. The next unit of value is therefore confidence, not another capability — outsiders must be able to identify the authoritative contract, reproduce behavior, understand limitations, and know who can decide a change.

## Decision
Adopt the recommendation of the Executive Decision Brief: CONDITIONAL GO, pending board ratification and the recorded completion of the Day Zero gate.

- **Standing diagnosis.** UOR gives digital and computational objects content-derived identity and portable verification; the operating system around it is immature. Public evidence posture is at best E2; no public E4–E6 evidence exists for Foundation claims.
- **Ten decisions (D1–D10).** The brief requests a single ratification-set covering: the authority chain; the public source of truth; release custody; volunteer capacity; the primary pilot; the evidence policy (public claims cite evidence, date, owner, and limits under the E0–E6 scale); the scope boundary; the escalations path; the maintenance posture; and the cycle termination condition.
- **Resolution.** The UOR Foundation authorizes a two-week Day Zero mobilization under a volunteer-only, no-funding assumption. The ninety-day baseline cycle may commence only after the Secretary records the Day Zero gate as complete. Until then, the portfolio remains in maintenance and governance preparation.
- **Go gate.** The Day Zero gate record must name at least three structured stewards with explicit accountability before Week 1 starts.
- **Feasibility envelope.** Planning range is 215–400 combined volunteer hours across thirteen weeks (≈17–31 hours/week); treated strictly as a capacity envelope, not a valuation of unpaid labor.
- **Risk posture.** Ten enterprise risks (R1–R10) are registered with owners and controls; the evidence policy (E0–E6 review before publication) is a hard control, not advisory.

## Consequences
* No ratification record = no baseline cycle; the portfolio stays in maintenance. The condition is mechanical, not negotiated.
* Public claims are bound to evidence grade, date, owner, and limits; unsupported claims degrade the Foundation's evidence posture on the record.
* The decision brief is the single ratifying artifact: approving it approves D1–D10, the Day Zero gate, and the risk register together.
* If any Day Zero condition fails, the Foundation continues maintenance and preparation rather than starting an under-gated cycle.
