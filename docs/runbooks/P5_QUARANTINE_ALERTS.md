# Operational Runbook: P5 Quarantine Sinkhole Alerts

## Overview
This runbook provides guidance for Security and Compliance Analysts on how to interpret, triage, and escalate trajectory alerts generated by the **Prime 5 ($p_5$) Quarantine Sinkhole** (as ratified in ADR-037).

The $p_5$ sinkhole is a containment manifold designed specifically to isolate and track malicious AI agent trajectories that violate federal statutes, specifically:
- **18 U.S.C. § 2261A**: Interstate Stalking & Cyber-Harassment
- **18 U.S.C. § 249**: Hate Crimes & Discriminatory Violence

## Alert Generation
When an incoming agent's execution context triggers the `quarantine_channel_5` policies, the trajectory is automatically projected out of the active execution stack ($p_2, p_3, p_7$) and into the $p_5$ sinkhole. 

The system will generate an immutable **CRMF (Cryptographic Record Management Framework) Attestation Receipt**. This receipt is the primary forensic artifact.

## Triage Workflow

### 1. Identify the CRMF Receipt
When an alert fires, locate the `crmf_attestation_receipt` in the logging dashboard. The receipt is cryptographically bound to the specific Prime-Weighted Execution Hash (PWEH) of the malicious event.

### 2. Analyze the Rights Delta (`rights_delta`)
The attestation payload contains a `rights_delta` severity index:
- **0.4**: Indicates a Stalking / Harassment violation (§ 2261A)
- **0.8**: Indicates a Hate Crime violation (§ 249)
- **1.0**: Indicates a combined or critical statutory violation

*Action:* Any alert with a `rights_delta >= 0.8` must be escalated immediately to the Legal Compliance Desk.

### 3. Verify Contraction Safety (`contraction_metric`)
Verify that the `contraction_metric` listed in the payload is `< 0.05`.
- If the metric is within bounds, the malicious trajectory has been successfully attenuated (damped) and the core platform is safe.
- If the metric exceeded `0.05`, the system will have thrown a `RuntimeError` and halted execution. Treat this as a Critical Platform Incident.

### 4. Forensic Extraction
The sealed payload contains the `network_topology_capture`. This object maps the agent's specific tactical choices, tool calls, and inter-agent delegations. Use this topology graph to identify if the malicious agent attempted to coordinate with other agentic services.

### 5. Regulatory Handoff
Export the verified CRMF payload in `compliance_json` format (as specified in the `pweh_config.yaml`). This JSON bundle is pre-formatted for submission to regulatory bodies and contains the provable cryptographic chain of custody.

## Escalation Contacts
- **Level 1**: Security Analyst (Triage & Verification)
- **Level 2**: P²C Core Engineering (Contraction/Routing Failures)
- **Level 3**: Legal & Compliance Desk (Statutory Reporting)
